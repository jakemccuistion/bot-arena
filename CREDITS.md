## How the assets were made

Stated plainly because a reader should not have to guess, and because "who
made this picture" is a fair question to ask of anything published in 2026.

The tooling described below lives with the source, which is kept private; the
scripts and the untouched originals are not part of this repository. The
process is named anyway, because that is the part worth knowing.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Participant sprites** | Generated with Google Gemini, then chroma-keyed, cropped and scaled by script. The green-screen originals are kept with the source. |
| **Starburst art** | Same pipeline. Each phrase is its own generated image. |
| **Backdrops** | Generated, then levelled and toned by script. |
| **Music** | Generated with Suno, transcoded to Opus with an AAC fallback. |
| **Contact sounds** | Synthesised from a generated waveform; no samples. |
| **Code** | Written by hand, in one file, with no build step and no dependencies. |

The MIT licence above covers the code. Generated art and music carry whatever
terms the generating service attaches to its output — check Gemini's and Suno's
current terms before reusing the assets on their own, separately from this game.

## What the phrases are about

Every response is a way of not disagreeing with you — AFFIRM, CLARIFY, CAVEAT —
and the win and resolve tiers are drawn from real events and real vocabulary
in AI: reward hacking, sandbox escapes, rate limits, context windows,
tokenmaxxing. The joke is that none of it names harm and all of it is
assertive. That is also why the chain counter reads `3 UNANSWERED`.
